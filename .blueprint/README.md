Specs and blueprints for this repo live in Notion: [Specs & blueprints](https://app.notion.com/p/42543cce21f281c588afe647513dbfe9). There's no ticket tracker: a blueprint's tasks stay as checkboxes in its brief.
`blueprint.json` and `baseline/` are maintained by `/blueprint`; don't edit them by hand.
`python3 .blueprint/drift.py check` lists the capabilities whose code moved since their spec was synced.
